Simple rules, composed: event timing × input shape × phone-pattern structure × sampled network × spatial observation. The speech-driven option has three causal inputs: sustained phone-duration vectors; brief exponential onset pushes; or gradually overlapping inputs from exponential smoothing. Independent patterns give each phone random detail. Shared patterns add four random common components while retaining independent detail. These are engineering priors, not fitted articulatory or neural responses.
