The whole pipeline

Sample a rule and an input. Simulate activity. Turn that activity into voltage at a sampled electrode grid. Apply the frontend and save the model’s input.

A “world” is one sampled setup: its rule, connections, input mapping and source maps stay fixed while its activity changes. Each fresh training world produces one recording.

Normalization and provenance

Fit each synthetic recording’s first 24 s with lower median / scaled MAD. Leave an 8 s buffer. Retain seconds 32–64; floor 10⁻⁶; cap ±15. Do not normalize again. The current real-data whole-record fit has a different scope.
